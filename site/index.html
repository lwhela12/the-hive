H.I.V.E.

Architects of Collective Abundance

Where brilliant minds don’t just network—they build.

Who we are

We’re a bunch of like-minded people who like looking after each other and our community, and we get together once a month. Between us we have almost everything anyone needs — it just sits scattered across a lot of heads. HIVE pools it.

What HIVE stands for

Human

Inspired by Isaac Asimov’s Trap Door Spider Society, Natalie Walstead took the idea and modernised it for a faster world.

Insight

A hurdle for you might be someone else’s hobby. An insurmountable mountain for you might be somebody’s Tuesday.

Vision

The first HIVE is local to Las Vegas, but the vision is wider than that, and open to anyone. We’re stronger together.

Execution

We meet monthly to brainstorm and then actually do it. Join in person or virtually, and catch up in the app.

Every month

Three parts to a meeting

  • HIVE Hang — the part where we’re just people who like each other.
  • HIVE Help — we pick one help to make happen, and everyone puts something toward it.
  • Humdinger — we sharpen what you’re stuck on until it’s specific enough for somebody to grant.

Once a month, a group of friends met for dinner. Every member brought a guest — and the whole evening was spent drawing that one person out.

Asimov spent years at a monthly dinner club in New York. In his autobiography I, Asimov he wrote about it as the Trap Door Spider Society, and turned it into fiction as the Black Widowers.

Natalie Walstead took that idea and built on it — same structure, faster world, and a table that’s open to everyone.
